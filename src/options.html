Whose reactions you see

Your own reactions always stay visible. This filters what you see; your Public reactions remain public. The following list uses your connected GitHub handle and refreshes every five minutes.

Your backend

Use your own Cloudflare Worker

The shared reacji.to Worker supports everyone’s isolated Slack connections. A personal instance is optional. Deploy your Worker with its own Slack app, then enter its HTTPS origin here.

Your GitHub access token is sent to the selected Worker to verify your identity. Only use a Worker you trust. Integration connections, delivery records, and private screenshots stay on that instance. Switching backends does not move or delete them.

Private screenshots

Select Include screenshot in the picker to capture the visible tab, including any visible form fields. Screenshots are saved privately in your selected Worker backend and require your GitHub identity to retrieve. They are never committed to the public repository or uploaded to Slack.

Screenshots stay saved until you delete them. The limit is 100 files or 50 MB per account. Hold Option (Alt on Windows/Linux) and drag over text to attach a highlighted passage without a screenshot.

        Your private reactions

        Choose a private repository owned by your GitHub account. The app needs Contents: read and write on that repo only.

        On GitHub, select Only select repositories. If creating a repo, choose Private, then install the app on it. Return here and refresh the list.

        On by default. Once a repository is selected, new public reactions and changes are also copied to its public/ folder so you can run your own automations. Private reactions stay in data/. Public reactions remain public. Existing history is not imported; turning this off leaves existing copies in place.

        What happens when you react

        1. Hold down Option (Alt on Windows/Linux) and click page content, or use the eyes beside a post.
        2. Choose Public, Private, Slack, or Raycast in the emoji picker.
        3. Your reaction is tied to your GitHub identity. On dev-null, your signed-in account must match.
        4. The Worker commits your emoji to the public data repo or your selected private repo. Web placements also save the page URL, element path, position, and a short visible text excerpt.
        5. Public reactions appear on reacji.to/YOUR_GITHUB_HANDLE and on the page for other extension users.

        Reactions reattach when their saved target can be matched. If a page changes too much, the pin stays hidden. Browser settings pages and other extension-restricted pages cannot have reactions.

        Where your reacji live

        Public and Private GitHub reactions are saved as commits. Public reacji and their history are visible to everyone in our public repository ↗.

        Private reacji live in your chosen private repo, accessible only to you and any collaborators you may have invited.

        GitHub hosts your data, and the app has permission to read and write your reactions. We check that your repository is private before saving.

        Reacji on webpages also save the page URL, placement details, and a short text excerpt so they can appear in the same spot later.

        Removing a reacji removes it from view, but doesn’t erase its Git history.
